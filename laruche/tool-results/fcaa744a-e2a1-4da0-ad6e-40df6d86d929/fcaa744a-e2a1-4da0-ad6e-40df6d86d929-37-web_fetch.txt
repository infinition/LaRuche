Sprites List and Guide: All Types, Abilities, and Extraction Tips

By Michael Koczwara, Shailyn Cotten, Andrew Smith, +1.6k more

Updated: Jun 18, 2026 2:51pm UTC

Sprites are a huge part of Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 3. There are currently 11 different types of Sprites of varying rarity, and each has its own unique ability that affects your gameplay. This guide teaches you everything you need to know about Sprites in Fortnite CH7S3, including all types, abilities, and rarities, plus tips on how to get and extract Sprites successfully so you can Master them.

All of the Sprites (except TheBurntPeanut) have rare, Special variants that have an additional effect on top of its pre-existing one:

Gold: You gain additional XP from eliminations.

Gummy: You gain 10% more Sprite Dust when extracting.

Galaxy: You gain 20% more ammo when opening ammo boxes.

Special Sprites are rarer than regular ones, especially if the rarity of the base Sprite is already high. Good luck finding a Gold Zero Point Sprite quickly!

How to Find Sprites

Sprites are primarily found in chests in Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 3, though they also randomly appear across the map and can drop when eliminating opponents (if they have one equipped).

Once you've found a Sprite, interact with it to pick it up and, if you don't already have one equipped, it'll automatically equip to your back. To switch which Sprite you have equipped, simply highlight it in your inventory and use it.

Finding a Sprite isn't enough to add it to your collection, though - you'll need to extract it while it's equipped or in your inventory. More on that below.

Getting eliminations, opening chests and ammo boxes, and more will earn XP for your equipped Sprite. Each Sprite has five levels, and you'll need to Master Sprites by extracting them at max level to earn rewards.

How to Extract Sprites in Fortnite CH7S3

Once you've found a Sprite that you want to add to your Sprite collection, you must extract it; if you die before you've done so, you'll lose the Sprite (though if you lose an already-extracted Sprite, you can purchase it again from the Sprites menu using Sprite Dust). If you win a Victory Royale, any Sprites you have on you will automatically extract.

Sprite Dust is by extracting Sprites at an Extraction Site or by using a Portable Extractor. You'll earn Sprite Dust even if you extract a Sprite you've already got in your collection. You can also earn Sprite Dust as Sprite Mastery rewards, both at tier 3 and tier 5.

Sprite Dust is spent at Sprite Dust Services terminals found at Extraction Sites. Here, you can use your Sprite Dust to buy perks, such as XP (once daily), weapon upgrades, and more Portable Extractors.

You'll also need Sprite Dust to buy back any Sprites in your collection that you lose when you die.
